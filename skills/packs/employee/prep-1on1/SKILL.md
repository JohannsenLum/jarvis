## Procedure
1. Find the person page and the last notes in `one-on-ones/<person>/`.
2. Draft `one-on-ones/<person>/YYYY-MM-DD.md`: follow-ups from last time, wins since, blockers,
   questions to ask, career topic (from `career.md`).
3. Send the user the top three talking points.
